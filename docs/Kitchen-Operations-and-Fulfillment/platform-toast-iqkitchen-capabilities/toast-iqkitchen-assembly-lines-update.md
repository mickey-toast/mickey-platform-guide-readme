Toast IQ can update an existing assembly line. Editable fields include:

- Name
- Ordered list of prep stations on the line
- Archive status

Example queries:

- "Rename the Hot Line assembly line to Mains."
- "Add the Plating station to the end of the Hot Line assembly line."
- "Archive the Brunch Line assembly line."
