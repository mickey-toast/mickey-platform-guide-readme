Toast IQ can list prep stations at your restaurant or across multiple locations, including each station's name, whether tickets are always printed, expediter routing (**Prep station only**, **Prep station and expediter**, or **Expediter only**), and printer assignment. For more information about prep stations, see [Prep stations](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminKitchenDiningRoomReference.html#configPrepStationsPage).

Example queries:

- "List all prep stations."
- "Which prep stations are set to send to expediter only?"
- "Which prep stations have a printer assigned?"
- "Show me prep stations across all my locations."
