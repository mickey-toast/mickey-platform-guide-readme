Toast IQ can summarize your restaurant's kitchen configuration in a single response, including Autofire devices, active item routing rules, expediter settings, meal pacing options, expediter and prep station KDS devices, and printers. It can also answer narrower questions about individual KDS devices or the kitchen setup configuration (fulfillment settings, printing options, warning configurations, dining behavior emphasis, and fulfillment status text messaging).

To review the same information in Toast Web, see [KDS configuration quick reference](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminKdsConfigQuickRef.html) and [Kitchen](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminKitchenDiningRoomReference.html#adminKitchenSetupReference).

Example queries:

- "Give me a kitchen overview for this location."
- "How is my kitchen configured for order fulfillment?"
- "Which devices are configured for Autofire?"
- "What's the expo level configured here?"
- "Show me the kitchen setup configuration."
