Every change Toast IQ makes to your kitchen configuration follows the same pattern:

1. Toast IQ describes exactly what it is about to change and asks you to confirm.
2. After you confirm, Toast IQ applies the change to your kitchen configuration.

If a request is ambiguous, Toast IQ lists every matching entity and asks you to choose before making any edit. For example, if you ask to update a prep station name and several stations match the name across your locations, Toast IQ lists all matching stations.
