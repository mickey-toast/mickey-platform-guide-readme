On the Toast platform, you have two options for publishing your changes. You can publish the changes manually when you are ready for employees and guests to see them or you can schedule them to be published at a specific date and time in the future.

> 📘 Note
>
> Your restaurant must have the Restaurant Management Essentials, Restaurant Management Pro, or Restaurant Management Enterprise package, or the multi-location module, to access the scheduled publishing feature.

Both manual publishes and scheduled publishes will eventually be incorporated into the publishing center. In this current version, however, only scheduled publishes are supported. Until manual publishing is added to the publishing center, you must continue to use the existing workflows described in [Publishing updates to POS location configuration](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformPublishingOverview.html) for manual publishes.
