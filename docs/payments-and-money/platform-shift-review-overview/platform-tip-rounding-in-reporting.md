If you enable cash rounding for cash tip payouts, the rounded amounts appear in several reports in Toast Web and on the POS:

- **Shift review report**: Includes a **Cash rounding** row that shows the total cash amount rounded from the employee's shift. For more information, see [Accessing shift review](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformAccessingShiftReview.html).

   ![The Shift Review report showing cash rounding from tipouts.](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/media/platformShiftReviewCashRoundingZReport.png)
- **Z report**: Includes a **Cash Rounding** row that shows the total amount of cash rounded for the shift. For more information, see this [Toast Support article](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Close-Out-Day-Z-Report-Auto-Capture?lang=en_US).

   ![The Manager shift review report showing cash rounding details for employees.](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/media/shiftReviewCashRoundingManagerShiftReview.png)
- **Sales summary report**: Includes a **Cash rounding from tipouts** row that shows the total amount your location paid out from tip rounding across all employees. For more information, see this [Toast Support article](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Sales-Summary-Report).

   ![The Sales summary report showing the Cash rounding from tipouts line item.](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/media/shiftReviewCashRoundingSalesSummaryReport.png)
