Toast IQ can list which menu items and modifiers are out of stock at your session location, and can mark items in stock, out of stock, or set a specific quantity in bulk.

> ❗️ Important
>
> Stock lookups and updates apply only to your currently selected session location. Toast IQ does not show or change stock for other locations.

Examples:

- "What menu items are out of stock right now?"
- "Show me all out of stock items at this location."
- "What menu items are 86-ed right now?"
- "What bagels are out of stock currently?"
- "Mark the Caesar Salad and the Margherita Pizza out of stock."
- "Set the Pumpkin Soup to 12 servings remaining."
- "Put all of my soup items back in stock."
