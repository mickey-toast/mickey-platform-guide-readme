The [online ordering dashboard](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminOnlineOrderingDashboard.html) displays delivery settings, such as your delivery quote time and applicable throttling delays. From the dashboard, you can turn delivery on or off or add a delay.

For first-party delivery orders, the dashboard displays the delivery quote time. For TDS orders, the dashboard displays the takeout quote time plus the delivery time. For example, if a guest places an order at 2:00 PM with a 30 minute quote time and a 30 minute delivery time, the expected delivery time for that order is 3:00 PM.

> 📘 Note
>
> The delivery time is dynamic, meaning it changes based on the time of day, day of the week, guest location, and other criteria.
