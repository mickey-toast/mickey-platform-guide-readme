Toast IQ can list kitchen assembly lines, including each line's name, archive status, and the prep stations on it with their order. Multi-location lookups return assembly line configuration per location. For more information about assembly lines, see [Creating an assembly line](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformKDSCreatingAssemblyLines.html).

Example queries:

- "List the assembly lines for this restaurant."
- "Which prep stations are on the Hot Line assembly line?"
- "Show me assembly lines across all my locations."
