To make the changes you specify in Toast Web available to employees and guests, you must save and then publish them. The publishing center in Toast Web provides a centralized location where you can view information about configuration changes that have been published or are scheduled to be published at a future date and time.

The **Publishing center** has the following tabs:

- The **Schedule** tab, accessed by selecting **Toast account > Publishing > Publishing center - Schedule** in Toast Web, lets you view change sets. [Change sets](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformUnderstandingScheduledPublishingAndChangeSets.html) are collections of changes that are scheduled to be saved and published at a future date and time. You can also use the **Schedule** tab to get information about change sets that had issues during execution.
- The **History** tab, accessed by selecting **Toast account > Publishing > Publishing center - History** in Toast Web, lets you see changes that were successfully published.
