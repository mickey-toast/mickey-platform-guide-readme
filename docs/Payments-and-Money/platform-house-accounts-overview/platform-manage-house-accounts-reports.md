You can view an overview of charges, payments, and adjustments made to house accounts linked to your location. Go to **Finance > Payments > House accounts > Reports** to open the **Reports** page. On the **Reports** page, you can view and complete the following:

- Select a custom date range to filter house account activities.
- Select the **Refresh** link. This refreshes the data on the **Reports** page.
- Access the download **Report**link.
- Filter activities by source:

   - All
   - POS
   - Invoice
- View charges made to the house account

   - View and open the order charged to the house account
   - View the name of the house account owner
   - View the applicable customer number
   - View the order amount
   - View the tax amount for the order
   - View the applicable discount amount for the order
   - View the order source
   - View the first name and last initial of the employee who completed the charge to the house account
   - View the date and time of the order
- View payments made to the house account

   - View and open the order charged to the house account
   - View the name of the house account owner
   - View the applicable customer number
   - View the payment amount
   - View the payment type:

      - Cash
      - Credit
      - Gift cards

      > 📘 Note
      >
      > You can configure other payment methods on the **Other payment options** page in Toast Web. For more information, see [Payment workflows](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformPwfTenders.html).
   - View the order source
   - View the first name and last initial of the employee who completed the payment to the house account
   - View the date and time of the payment
- View adjustments made to the house account

   - View the name of the house account owner
   - View the applicable customer number
   - View the adjustment amount
   - View applicable notes
   - View the name of the employee who completed the adjustment to the house account
   - View the date and time of the adjustment

### Viewing house account reports

You can view your deferred revenue from house accounts in the **Sales summary** report on Toast Web. Go to **Reports > Sales > Sales summary** to open the **Sales summary** report. The **Deferred (house accounts)** row in the **Sales summary** report details the sum of payments made to the total outstanding house account balance on a given day or a specific time range. For more information about deferred revenue, see this [Toast Support article](https://support.toasttab.com/article/Configuring-Deferred-Sales-for-Gift-Certificates-and-Other-Menu-Items).
