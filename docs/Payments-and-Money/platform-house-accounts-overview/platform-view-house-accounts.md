You can view your active and archived house accounts in Toast Web. Go to **Finance > Payments > House accounts** to open the **Accounts** page. On the **Accounts** page, you can view the following information:

- Total number of house accounts
- Total outstanding balance of house accounts
- List of active and archived house accounts

On the **Accounts** page, you can complete the following actions:

- Bulk adjust house account balances. For more information, see [Bulk adjust house account balances](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformViewHouseAccounts.html#platformBulkAdjustHouseAccountBalances).
- Choose to create either a single or multiple house accounts
- Search for a house account by name or account number
- Use the date picker to search for active house accounts with transactions created within a time range

   > 📘 Note
   >
   > If a time range is selected, this populates the **Total Spend** column. The total spend is the total amount charged to a house account and the total amount invoiced to the account. For more information, see [Paying for orders with a house account](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformHouseAccountsPaymentMethod.html#platformPayWithHouseAccounts).
- Filter house accounts by outstanding balance or last transaction date
- Show and hide various table column options
- Download house accounts table details

![The Accounts page in Toast Web.](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/media/platform-house-accounts-accounts-page.png)

### Bulk adjust house account balances

You can choose to bulk adjust house account balances on the **Accounts** page in Toast Web.

**To bulk adjust house account balances**

1. [Access Toast Web](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminAccessToastAdminBackend.html).
2. Go to **Finance > Payments > House accounts > Accounts** to open the **Accounts** page.
3. On the **Accounts** page, check the box next to each house account you want to adjust the balance for. This displays a dialog.
4. Select the **Adjust balances** link in the dialog. This opens the **Adjust balances** dialog.
5. In the **Adjust balances** dialog, choose to adjust house account balances to $0.00 or to a custom amount.
6. Select the **Continue** button to save your changes and close the dialog.
7. The **Accounts** page displays the new outstanding balance that applies to the selected house accounts. Add a note for your accounting records as needed.
8. Select the **Apply adjustments** button to apply the adjustment. The **Accounts** page displays the house accounts with the new balances.
