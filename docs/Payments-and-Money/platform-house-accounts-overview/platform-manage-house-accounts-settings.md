You can configure house account settings on the **Settings** page in Toast Web. Go to **Finance > Payments > House accounts > Settings** to open the **Settings** page. On the **Settings** page, you can configure the following:

- POS house account payment settings:

   - Choose to enable house accounts as a payment method on a POS device. For more information, see [Enabling house accounts as a payment method](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformHouseAccountsPaymentMethod.html#platformEnableHouseAccountsPaymentMethod).

      > 📘 Note
      >
      > This setting must be enabled to show all other settings in this section.
   - Choose to show the tipping screen for house account payments. This setting allows guests to add a tip when paying with a house account.
   - Choose to require guest signatures for house account payments. If configured, set the payment amount that requires a signature.
   - Choose to prevent all employees from viewing a guest’s house account balance when processing a house account payment.
   - Choose to allow employees to select how to share the receipt after processing a house account payment.
   - Choose to prompt employees to ask guests to join available rewards programs when paying with a house account.
   - Choose to show guests a summary of the house account payment before completing the payment.
   - Choose to set a limit on the amount that can be charged to a house account. Any amount that exceeds the set limit is charged as a separate payment.

      - Choose to not allow a positive house account balance.
      - Choose to allow a positive house account balance.
- House account invoice settings:

   - Select a default dining option. Choose a dining option to filter POS orders linked to a house account. For more information, see [Managing house account invoices](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformManageHouseAccounts.html).
   - Set a default time range to generate new house account invoices.
   - Choose to include receipts with house account invoices.
   - Choose to create open invoices without sending email notifications to guests.
   - Choose to allow guests to save a payment card to their house account. For more information, see [Managing house account information](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformManageHouseAccountsInfo.html).
