Below is a list of emails sent to customers who are subscribed to your subscription plan. This list is for reference only and may change.

### Subscription plan confirmation email

This email confirms that you successfully signed up for the subscription plan.

![An example of the subscription plan confirmation email.](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/media/platform-subscriptions-plan-confirmation-email.png)

### Subscription plan renewal email

This email confirms that your subscription is set to be renewed on a specific date.

![An example of the subscription renewal email.](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/media/platform-subscriptions-plan-renewal-email.png)

### Failed subscription payment email

This email notifies you that your last payment could not be processed.

![An example of the failed subscription payment email.](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/media/platform-subscriptions-plan-failed-payment-email.png)

### Skip subscription email

This email confirms that you successfully skipped your subscription's next order.

![An example of the skip subscription email.](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/media/platform-subscriptions-plan-skip-payment-email.png)

### Subscription plan request cancellation confirmation email

This email confirms that you submitted a request to cancel your subscription.

![An example of the subscription plan request cancellation confirmation email.](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/media/platform-subscriptions-plan-cancellation-email.png)

### Subscription plan cancellation confirmation email

This email confirms that your subscription has been canceled.

![An example of the subscription plan cancellation confirmation email.](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/media/platform-subscriptions-plan-canceled-email.png)

### Limited-time subscription plan expiration email

This email confirms that your limited-time subscription plan is set to expire on a specific date.

![An example of the limited-time subscription plan expiration confirmation email.](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/media/platform-subscriptions-plan-limited-plan-email.png)

### Add a payment method email

This email requests you add a payment method to your profile.

![An example of the add a payment method email.](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/media/platform-subscriptions-plan-payment-method-email.png)
