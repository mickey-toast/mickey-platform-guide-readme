You can archive a service charge, which means the service charge becomes inactive and is not applied to checks. Archived service charges are not displayed on the **Service charges** page, unless you select the **Show Archived** link.

**To archive a service charge**

1. [Access Toast Web](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminAccessToastAdminBackend.html).
2. Choose **Finance > Related > Settings > Service charges & fees** to open the **Service charges** page.
3. Select the service charge to be archived.
4. Select the **Action** button (in the right-most column) and select **Archive**.
5. Select **Save**.
6. Select the **Publish Now** link.
