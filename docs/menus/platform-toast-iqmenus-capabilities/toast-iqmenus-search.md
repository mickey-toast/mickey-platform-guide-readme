Toast IQ can search across your menus, menu groups, menu items, modifier groups, and modifiers and return details such as name, pricing, availability, visibility, modifiers, taxes, prep stations, and reporting metadata. Searches are read-only and do not change anything.

Toast IQ can scope a search to a specific menu or menu group, match approximate or partial names (for example, finding "Cheese Burger" when you ask about "burgers"), search across multiple locations in one request, and return counts without listing every match.

Examples of questions you can ask:

- "What's the price of the Margherita Pizza?"
- "List all menu items in the Lunch menu."
- "Which menus does the Sides menu group appear in?"
- "What prep stations are used by my Quesadilla menu item?"
- "How many vegan items do I have on the dinner menu?"
