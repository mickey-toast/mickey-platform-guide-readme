> 📘 Note
>
> Currently, the allergens feature is only available for Toast Tables. For more information, see this [Toast Support article](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Getting-Started-Toast-Waitlist).

On the **Toast Tables** page in Toast Web, you can configure your reservation settings and enable the **Allergens** setting. The setting lets your guests choose from a list of allergens when they book a reservation.

**To configure guest allergens**

1. [Access Toast Web](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminAccessToastAdminBackend.html).
2. Go to **Waitlist & Reservations > Settings > General > Reservation form** to open the **Allergens** section.
3. Under the **Allergens** section, use the drop-down menu to select the allergens guests can choose from when booking a reservation.

   > 📘 Note
   >
   > If you choose to add an allergen not listed, the allergen will be available for your guest to choose from when making a reservation, however that allergen will not appear on kitchen tickets.

   ![This image shows the Allergens dropdown menu on the Toast Tables page in Toast Web.](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/media/guest-allergies-toast-tables-settings.png)
4. Select the **Save changes**button to save your changes.
5. Go to the **Toast account > Publishing > Publishing center** page to publish your changes. For more information, see [Publishing center overview](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformPublishingCenterOverview.html).

   Your guests will be able to choose from a pre-selected list of allergens when they book a reservation on Toast Tables.
