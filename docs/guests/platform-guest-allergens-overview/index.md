Certain types of food can cause an allergic reaction. Allergic reactions can result in severe, potentially fatal symptoms caused by accidental exposure or cross-contamination. The allergens feature lets guests notify restaurant staff of known allergens when booking a reservation using Toast Tables. This allows staff to use safe food preparation methods, while ensuring compliance with safety laws.
