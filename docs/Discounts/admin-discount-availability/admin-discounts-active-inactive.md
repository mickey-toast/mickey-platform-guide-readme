When you create a new discount, the discount is active by default.

On the **Discounts** page, you use the toggle in the **Active** column to control whether the discount is inactive.

An inactive discount does not display on the Toast POS app and is not applied automatically.

To deactivate a discount, slide the **Active** toggle switch to the left.

To activate a discount, slide the **Active** toggle switch to the right.

After you make a discount active or inactive, select **Save** to save the status change, then [publish the changes](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformPublishingOverview.html).
